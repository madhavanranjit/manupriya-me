Privacy Policy for Defervescence

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Defervescence ("the App") is developed and operated by MaatriAI Solutions Private Limited ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Defervescence, you agree to the practices described here.

Jump to: How to delete your account and data →

1. Who this app is for

Defervescence is designed to help parents and caregivers track a child's fever readings and medicine administration over time. It is intended to be used by adults on behalf of a child in their care.

2. Information we collect

Account information. When you sign in using Google Sign-In, we receive your name, email address, and Google account ID to create and secure your account.

Health tracking information you enter. This includes:

  • Temperature readings and timestamps, entered manually or by voice
  • Medicine name, dose, and time administered, entered manually, by voice, or via quick-add
  • An optional name or pseudonym you choose to enter for the person being tracked — you are never required to enter a real name, and we recommend using a pseudonym if you prefer

Voice recordings. If you use the voice entry feature to log a temperature or medicine, your voice recording is processed to extract the relevant details (such as the reading or medicine name and dose) and is not stored after processing.

We do not knowingly collect information directly from children. All information is entered by the adult using the App.

3. How we use this information

  • To let you log, view, and track fever and medicine history over time
  • To sync your data across your devices when you sign in
  • To operate and improve the App's core functionality
  • To generate a fever/medicine report that you may choose to share yourself via WhatsApp, print, or save as a PDF — this sharing happens only when you actively choose to do so, and is sent directly from your device
  • To communicate with you about your account, if needed

We do not use the health information you enter for advertising, and we do not sell your data to third parties.

4. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored using Supabase (database and authentication infrastructure) and served via Vercel (hosting infrastructure). These providers act as our data processors and are contractually and technically responsible for securing the infrastructure on which your data resides.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal or health information. We may share information only:

  • With service providers who help us operate the App (such as our hosting and database providers), solely to provide the service
  • If required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of MaatriAI Solutions, our users, or others

6. How to delete your account and data

To delete your Defervescence account and all associated data:

  1. Send an email to maatriaisolutions@gmail.com from the email address linked to your Google Sign-In
  2. Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request"
  3. We will confirm your request and delete your account and data within 7 business days

What gets deleted: your account information (name, email, Google account ID), all fever and temperature readings, all medicine entries, any patient names or pseudonyms you entered, and any reports you generated.

What may be retained: we aim to delete your data promptly upon a verified request. In limited cases we may retain: (a) transaction records, if you made any in-app purchases, as required under Indian tax and accounting law, and (b) minimal technical logs briefly, for fraud and abuse prevention. No fever, medicine, or patient information is retained once your request has been processed.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You may use a pseudonym instead of a child's real name when logging entries
  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time — see how to delete your account and data above

8. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account remains active, or as needed to provide the App's functionality. If you request account deletion, we follow the process and retention limits described above.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and access controls. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's privacy

Defervescence is intended for use by adults tracking a child's health information, not for direct use by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through account creation or app use.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date above when changes are made. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
