Our products

Tools built for the last mile.

Each product addresses a specific, unmet clinical need in low-resource Indian healthcare — pragmatic, affordable, and designed to scale.

Defervescence
Smart fever tracker · Climate-driven outbreak detection
An AI-assisted mobile app that helps caregivers monitor and interpret fever in children — and aggregates anonymous, geo-tagged data to detect climate-driven infectious disease outbreaks before they reach hospital systems. Designed for ASHAs and community health workers with offline capability.
Android app React · Capacitor · Supabase Usability trial ongoing

MaatriAI
Antenatal record interpretation · High-risk pregnancy identification
An AI tool that reads photographs of paper antenatal records — blood reports, urine tests, ultrasounds, doctors' notes — and generates colour-coded summaries for patients and CHOs. Flags missing investigations, highlights abnormal parameters, calculates risk for eclampsia and GDM, and delivers audio guidance in local languages. No manual data entry. Built for ASHA workers under NHM/RMNCH+A workflows.

Glyco
Voice-based glucose monitoring · Home screen widget
A voice-first glucose logging app with a home screen widget for quick, frictionless recording — designed for diabetic patients who need to log readings multiple times daily without unlocking their phone. Roadmap includes an IoT glucometer that pushes readings directly to the app, eliminating manual entry entirely.
